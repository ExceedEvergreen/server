About the author

Author Name grew up between two coastlines, which may explain why so much of the work returns to the idea of leaving. Trained first as a journalist, the shift into fiction came from wanting to sit longer with the questions the news cycle moved past too quickly.

Their debut novel was named a best book of the year by three major outlets and has been translated into twelve languages. When not writing, Author Name teaches a small fiction workshop and spends an unreasonable amount of time rearranging the same bookshelf.
